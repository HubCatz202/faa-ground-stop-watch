How to use this tracker

The card above says whether the ground stop is still in effect and when the feed was last checked. Timeline files every update under a numbered event: tap an event to see only that one, or Official, News or Social to see one kind of source. Trending ranks what people are talking about, and the cards move as attention shifts. A fresh pass over news and social sources runs about every 5 minutes and new entries appear on their own, so there is no need to reload. Anything marked Unverified has not been confirmed by an official source. How it works, in the README
