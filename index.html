How to use this tracker

The card above says whether the ground stop is still in effect and when the feed was last checked. Timeline lists every update in time order with the newest at the top; tap Official, News or Social to see one kind of source. Trending ranks what people are talking about; tap a card to compare what officials, news outlets and social posts are each saying on that topic. A fresh pass over news and social sources runs about every 5 minutes and new entries appear on their own, so there is no need to reload. Tap any entry to open its source. Anything marked Unverified has not been confirmed by an official source. How it works, in the README
